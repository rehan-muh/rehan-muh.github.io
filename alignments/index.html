alignments

Ancestor–descendant segment alignments behind a cross-linguistic study of word position and sound change.

Each table is one cognate set. The top row is the ancestor word, divided into segments, with ˈ before the stressed syllable where the stress is known. Each row below gives, under every ancestor segment, the descendant segment that continues it, or - where it was lost. Segments a descendant added have no ancestor segment and are not shown. Alignments of datasets that do not come with segment alignments were made by a neural aligner trained on hand-aligned data and then reviewed by hand.

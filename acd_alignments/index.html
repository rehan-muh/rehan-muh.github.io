acd alignments

Every reflex in the cognate sets of the Austronesian Comparative Dictionary (Blust, Trussel and Smith), aligned segment by segment with the reconstruction that heads its set.

The reconstructions and reflexes were converted from the dictionary's spelling to IPA segments, with each language's spelling read as the dictionary cited for it describes it. Each reflex was aligned with its reconstruction automatically, and every alignment was then read by a large language model acting as a reviewer, which confirmed it, corrected it, or marked a problem. No person has read the alignments.

Source: Blust, Robert, Stephen Trussel, and Alexander D. Smith. The Austronesian Comparative Dictionary. CLDF dataset lexibank/acd, licence CC BY 4.0. Please cite the dictionary when using these tables.

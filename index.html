Muhammad Rehan

[mɔːˈhʌmʌd reɪˈhan] · PhD student in the Program in Indo-European Studies at UCLA.

I am a 4th year PhD student in the Program in Indo-European Studies at UCLA. Trained as a historical linguist, Indo-Europeanist, and a phonologist, I am broadly interested in the evolution and spatial distributions of phonological variables. Much of my research applies Bayesian statistical methods to model the spatial, temporal, and phylogenetic dynamics of phonological variables. In parallel, I develop integrative phylogenetic methods that incorporate phonological characters into phylogenetic inference, and apply them to questions in Indo-European and other language families. I am also interested in developing large-scale analyses of phonetic evidence, including experimental data, to understand cross-linguistic variation in sound systems. As an Indo-Europeanist, I work on the historical phonology and philology of the ancient Indo-European languages, and I bring the same spatial and computational methods to questions in the digital humanities.

Please look around and feel free to contact me with questions about any of my work and requests for code. I am also very happy to collaborate on projects if you see something on the site that catches your eye!

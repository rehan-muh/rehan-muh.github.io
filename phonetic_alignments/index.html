How far to trust the automatic phone boundaries

A ranking of the 229 forced-aligned (source, language) sets of the cross-linguistic V(C)V dataset by how sure one can be that their phone boundaries are right. The hand-segmented corpora are the reference and are not ranked. Each set gets a score out of 100 from five parts: what produced the alignment, the recording and speech conditions, how speakers are identified, two internal quality signals computed from the aligned data, and, where it exists, external evidence (a token audit or agreement with a hand-segmented corpus of the same language).

Provenance classes, most to least trustworthy

1. VoxCommunis published MFA alignments74
48 sets · 232 h · 1,819,244 sequences · scores 68–85

MFA acoustic model and lexicon built per language on the Common Voice data itself; TextGrids distributed with speaker cleaning. Audio is MP3 crowd speech, which costs in conditions.

2. DoReCo WebMAUS, word boundaries hand-checked73
42 sets · 68 h · 464,764 sequences · scores 69–82

Published alignment of fieldwork narratives; word boundaries were checked by the depositors, phone boundaries were not. Spontaneous speech, variable recording quality.

3. MFA pretrained language model (aligned here)73
33 sets · 181 h · 1,701,339 sequences · scores 62–84

MFA's own per-language acoustic model + pronunciation dictionary. On identical English audio these boundaries fall within 20 ms of hand-corrected ones for 97 % of vowels (L2-ARCTIC check).

4. VoxCommunis model reused on another corpus (aligned here)64
20 sets · 44 h · 367,519 sequences · scores 45–73

A Common Voice-trained model and lexicon applied to a different corpus (FLEURS, OpenSLR, VoxPopuli); domain shift, lexicon coverage limits.

5. MFA trained here, ≥ 5 h and a real lexicon61
3 sets · 19 h · 111,555 sequences · scores 56–64

Acoustic model trained on the corpus being aligned with at least 5 hours; pronunciations from a curated lexicon.

6. MFA trained here, small corpus or G2P lexicon55
12 sets · 28 h · 170,496 sequences · scores 29–62

Model trained on under 5 hours, or pronunciations generated by G2P (epitran) rather than a lexicon. Audit of two such FLEURS sets found misplaced boundaries in 40-100 % of sequences.

7. Pangloss pooled model (≤ 0.75 h per language)45
29 sets · 30 h · 255,548 sequences · scores 39–53

One acoustic model pooled over ~30 fieldwork languages from sentence-level IPA, then applied per language. Audit: 40 % (Puragi) and 90 % (Moi) of sequences had a misplaced boundary.

8. VoxClamantis Kaldi CTM, 10 ms grid, released 11-point formants45
42 sets · 18 h · 166,863 sequences · scores 36–49

Cross-language Kaldi alignment on a 10 ms grid with G2P phone tiers; only the released eleven-point formant summaries exist, no 1 ms frames, no consonants.

The number on each card is the median score of its sets. Classes are ordered by that median; within a class, sets differ by conditions, speaker identity, the internal signals and external evidence.

Internal QA2012 × (1 − (share of vowel tokens ≤ 30 ms − 0.08) / 0.5, clipped to 0–1) + 8 × (1 − implausible-frame share / 0.03, clipped). Very short vowels are the typical signature of an aligner that could not place a segment; implausible frames are F1/F2 outside 150–1400 / 400–3500 Hz. Sets without a vowel-token table take their class median and are flagged.
External evidence15Token audit: 15 × (1 − share of audited sequences with a misplaced boundary). Cross-corpus check: 15 × ((mean rz of per-vowel F1/F2 medians against a hand-segmented corpus of the same language − 0.85) / 0.14, clipped). Both present: their mean. Neither: 6, so unverified sets sit in the middle rather than at either end.

Reference points behind the priors: on the same L2-ARCTIC audio, MFA's pretrained English model puts 97 % of vowel onsets within 20 ms of the hand-corrected boundary (median move 10 ms); the audited forced sets had misplaced boundaries in 0–20 % of sequences for DoReCo and VoxCommunis Tamil, 40 % for VoxCommunis Swahili, FLEURS Cebuano and Pangloss Puragi, and 90–100 % for Pangloss Moi and FLEURS Afrikaans (models trained on under an hour). The score is a judgement aid, not a measurement: use the ranked evidence columns, and prefer the hand-segmented corpora for anything boundary-sensitive.
